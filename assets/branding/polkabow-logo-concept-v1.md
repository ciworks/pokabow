## Design rationale

- Warm gold, royal blue, ivory faces, and strong outlines reflect the existing castle backgrounds, king avatars, and tile artwork.
- The crown links the identity to the royal theme; Ace/spade and King/heart tiles communicate poker.
- Red, blue, green, and yellow edge bands reference the distinctive edge-matching mechanic.
- Bold lettering provides a clear primary title for splash screens, hub artwork, and promotional use.
- This is decorative brand art, not an illustration of a legal board move. Its decorative tile edge segmentation is not a gameplay tile template.

## Review and limitations

The title spelling and visible suit/rank symbols were visually inspected. PNG dimensions and sampled transparent pixels were checked. This is a raster concept, not vector artwork. Small-header and app-icon variants should simplify the emblem and decoration; no in-game readability test or scene integration has been performed. Existing title labels and artwork are unchanged.

## Updated brief for a future full-size logo (not yet generated)

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: primary mobile game title logo, standalone transparent PNG concept, wide approximately 2:1 composition.
Primary request: Design an original polished logo for the existing game POKABOW, a friendly competitive mobile tile-placement game blending poker hands with colored tile-edge matching on a board.
Art direction derived from inspected project artwork: illustrated royal castle setting, deep royal blue banners, rich warm gold bevels, ivory tile faces, saturated red/blue/green/yellow tile edges, strong clean dark outlines, approachable storybook king character. Match that polished illustrated mobile board-game aesthetic.
Subject and composition: one cohesive compact logo. Dominant exact wordmark "POKABOW" in one line, all seven letters clearly legible P O K A B O W. Custom bold slightly playful block-serif lettering, warm ivory-to-gold faces, restrained golden bevel, deep royal-blue extrusion and dark outer keyline for readability over both light and dark scenes. Broad uncomplicated letter silhouettes and generous counters; lettering is the hero. Above the middle of the wordmark, a small three-point gold crown and two overlapping substantial square game tiles form one compact emblem, not a separate floating illustration. The tiles have ivory faces, softly rounded corners, thick segmented red, blue, green and yellow edge bands. One tile clearly shows A and a black spade; the other shows K and a red heart. The colored edges are a distinctive part of the identity. Keep tile faces orderly and uncluttered. A very restrained blue-and-gold curved base may unify the wordmark if helpful.
Style: beautifully finished game-brand illustration with crisp vector-like contours, controlled dimensional shading, strong silhouette, minimal small details. Regal, warm, strategic and playful. Readable on mobile at small sizes. Avoid exaggerated ornate crests.
Background: truly transparent alpha, no opaque canvas, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no scenery. Center the complete logo with clear padding, nothing cropped.
Text verbatim: POKABOW. Only additional text is A and K on the two tiles. No tagline, no labels.
Avoid: dice, coins, poker chips, roulette, slot-machine imagery, money symbols, piles of gems, weapons, king portraits, extra ranks or numbers, tiny decorative suit scatter, intricate filigree, heavy sparkles, blurry glow, photographic mockup, watermark. Do not add any text besides the specified wordmark and tile ranks.
Produce one primary logo, not a sheet of variants.
