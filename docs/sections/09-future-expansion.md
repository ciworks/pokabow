# 09 - Future Expansion

**Status:** Future. None of the items below are part of the base game.
**Source of truth:** `docs/GAME_SPEC.md` section 11.

The base game must not be over-engineered to accommodate these, but the
architecture should not unnecessarily prevent them (`AGENTS.md` sections 11 and
23).

---

## 1. Scoring

Future scoring mechanics:

- Optional multipliers.
- Power tiles.
- Board multipliers.
- Combo bonuses.

These are explicitly **not** part of the base scoring system.

---

## 2. AI

- Adaptive difficulty.
- More advanced turn planning.

---

## 3. Content

- Campaign / puzzle boards.
- New tile faces.
- New edge patterns.

---

## 4. Multiplayer

- PvP multiplayer mode.

---

## 5. Meta / Cosmetics

- Cosmetic unlocks.
- Additional visual effects.

---
