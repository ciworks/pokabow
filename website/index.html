Gameplay

Built for the long game

Build Your Hand

Place tiles strategically to create high-scoring combinations. Pair, two pair, three of a kind, four of a kind, and straights all count - and the edges you match add to every one of them.

[GAMEPLAY SCREENSHOT 1]Deck row with a staged line

Turn-Based Play

Take your turn when you're ready. A match continues across sessions, so nothing is lost if you put the phone down mid-thought.

[GAMEPLAY SCREENSHOT 2]Match resumed mid-turn

Big Scoring Moments

A scoring move shows you exactly which tiles earned, group by group, while the points rise beside them. Hand order is animated so you can read it at a glance.

The Rival

Three difficulties: Easy plays a random legal move, Medium plays the best immediate score, and Hard plans ahead and will block the line you wanted.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How does a match work?

Players take turns placing tiles on a shared 12 by 12 board. Each turn you stage one or more tiles, rotate them to match the edges around them, then confirm the line. Points come from the poker-style hands those tiles form, from matching edge colours, and from connected colour clusters.

How long is a game?

It depends how full the board gets. The match ends when the board fills or when neither player has a tile left and the supply is empty. You can stop at any point - your turn resumes exactly where you left it.

Can I play against friends?

Not yet. The launch build is you against the rival AI at three difficulty levels. Friend challenges and online play are on the roadmap, not in this version.
